Privacy for approval rooms

Receipts records the IP address of approval attempts and approval events to prevent abuse and protect room links. This data is retained for up to 30 days, then purged. We also retain approval decisions, comments, and immutable Buffer snapshots for the creator’s approval record. If a creator connects their Buffer account, Receipts stores that connection’s access and refresh tokens encrypted on the server so it can sync their posts and the notes their team writes on them in Buffer; disconnecting deletes them. Buffer notes stay visible only to the creator unless they choose to share one in a room. Comments, change requests and approvals you leave in an approval room are also copied onto the creator’s Buffer post as a note, with your name and company, so their team sees them in Buffer.

Approval rooms are private links. Do not share them publicly. Contact the creator who sent your room link with privacy questions.
